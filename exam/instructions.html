Important rules

  • The timer starts when you click “Start Examination”.

  • The exam submits automatically when the timer reaches zero.
  • After submission, you will see your score, answer review, and chapter-wise performance.
  • A pass certificate can be printed or saved as a PDF if you meet the pass threshold.
