FOUNDER & CREATOR

Anowar Ali Mondal

I am a B.Sc. Medical Laboratory Technology (BMLT) student in Allied Health Sciences at Sister Nivedita University. Alongside my academic journey, I’m interested in technology, digital tools and building practical online resources that can help people complete everyday tasks more easily.

My interests include exploring web technology, creating helpful digital resources and making common online tasks easier to understand. Anowar Creation is where I bring these interests together while continuing to learn, build and improve.

That interest inspired me to create Anowar Creation: a portal that brings service information, document assistance, PDF utilities, assignment front pages, and online examination access into one familiar place.

MY JOURNEY

Learning by building useful things

For me, Anowar Creation is both a useful service portal and an ongoing learning project. I work on making the pages easier to navigate, the service information clearer, and the customer experience more organised from the first visit through the order update.

The platform includes application-related guidance for PAN and Voter Card services, scholarship support, DL/RC assistance, PDF printing, Front Page Maker tools, and a separate Online Exam portal. I want these resources to be easy to discover without making users search through many unrelated websites.

My approach is to keep learning, make practical improvements, communicate clearly and take genuine feedback seriously. The goal is not to claim perfection, but to make the platform more helpful and dependable as it grows.
